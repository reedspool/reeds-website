To Enter The Unknown Is Uncomfortable

Always.

Genre gives you expectations.

Rules give you guidelines and hints.

Everything which lessens the unknown-ness is helpful.

Everything which lessens the unknown-ness restricts the possibilities.

These are promises that you will be safe.

Maybe it's really about trust and safety.

Would you trust me that I will keep you safe?

Would you trust a friend that they will keep you safe?

Would you trust a group of strangers that they will keep you safe?

To really know what's in a room you must be in the room. So you must enter.
