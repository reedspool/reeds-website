# Materials

## Required

- at least 10 blank index cards per person
- a pen/pencil per person

## Fun

Any and all craft materials

- Crayons
- Markers
- Stickers
- Dice
- Scissors
- Tape
- Markers
- Poker chips

If you have them, they will get used.

Materials may be included into the gameplay. Fun.

## Space

The game will fill the space provided. And often more. You may like a big clear table.
