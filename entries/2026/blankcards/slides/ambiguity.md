All rules are ambiguous.

Life is an infinite game with infinite possibilities.

All instructions are ambiguous.

"How to Make a Peanut Butter and Jelly Sandwich"

How do we survive?

We fill in the blanks. Very quickly.

We can use this as a tool.

One person writes a card.

When another person looks at it, they fill in the blanks.

They share what they filled in. And it works.

"You play it, you say it" - Ryan's Rule

We do not go search for the original author's intent. The original author is ~~dead~~ blank.

I also ask to update the card, so we don't fill-in-the-blanks differently later.
