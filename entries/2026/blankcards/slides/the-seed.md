1000 Blank Cards needs a seed. A root. A jumping-off-point. Some guidance.

But there are infinite possible seeds.

1. I play with a certain seed that I wrote from guesses and research.
2. I hope it makes a game I want to play.
3. I hope the game I want to play is one others want to play with me.
4. I change, play, and refine the seed based on experiences and theories.

I recommend this order of priorities.

---

My seed goes on cards so they can be fucked with.

I prioritize communication.

I highlight and welcome ambiguity.

I maximize the fear of the unknown

I like all that. You may not.

What's your seed?
