> In case you're confused, here's a quick primer on my four prescriptive interactive forms: the base interactive system with no goals is a "toy". Add an objective/solution and you get a puzzle. Add measurement and you get a contest. Obfuscate game information (allowing for decisions to exist), and you get a game (a contest of decision-making).

- [Keith Burgun on game developer dot com](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/videogames-are-broken-toys)
