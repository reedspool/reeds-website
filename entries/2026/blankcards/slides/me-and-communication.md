title: Me and Communication

I have a thing about communication.

I get worked up at miscommunication.

When two people are talking and nodding but actually they are not hearing each other.

When two people are using the same words but meaning different things.

When two people can't bring themselves to talk about what's important.

Worse when it's more than two people.

So the seed of the game I have made is for me. I run the game I want to play for me.
